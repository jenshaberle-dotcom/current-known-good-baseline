# Framework Language

CKGB may use NIST AI RMF and ISO/IEC 42001 language from the start.

NIST AI RMF framing:

- Govern
- Map
- Measure
- Manage

ISO/IEC 42001 framing:

- AI management system
- risk and opportunity management
- documented information
- performance evaluation
- continual improvement

This repository does not claim certification or compliance.
