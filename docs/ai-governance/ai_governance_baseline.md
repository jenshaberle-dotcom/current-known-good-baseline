# AI Governance Baseline

AI-related projects should evaluate whether they need:

- NIST AI RMF mapping
- ISO/IEC 42001 mapping
- AI risk register
- control catalog
- human oversight model
- authorization scope
- side-effect boundary
- evidence chain
- drift categories
- provider/API budget guard

These are not always required at project start. They are part of the 200% master template and must be selected based on project risk.
